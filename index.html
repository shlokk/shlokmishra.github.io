Shlok Mishra

I'm a Senior AI Research Scientist at Meta AI in Menlo Park, where I lead the team for post-training of multimodal LLMs with image/video generation. Before this, I was an AI Research Scientist at Meta working on large-scale pre-training of LLMs and vision encoders, and did four research internships at Google (DeepMind, Google Research, Google AR) in self-supervised learning and generative modeling. I received my PhD from the University of Maryland College Park in 2023 where I was advised by Prof. David Jacobs.

Research

I am interested in computer vision and machine learning. My current research focuses on multimodal foundation models: large-scale vision encoders, multimodal LLMs, and unified models for visual understanding and generation.

Unifying Contrastive and Generative Objectives for Visual Understanding and Text-to-Image Generation
Chao Li*, Tianhong Li, Sai Vidyaranya Nuthalapati, Hong-You Chen, Satya Narayan Shukla, Jianpeng Cheng, Yonghuan Yang, Jun Xiao, Xiangjun Fan, Aashu Singh, Dina Katabi, Shlok Kumar Mishra
NeurIPS 2026

We introduce DREAM, a unified framework that reconciles text-image contrastive and generative objectives via masking warmup, so a single encoder excels at both understanding and generation.

RepFusion: Leveraging Multimodal Priors for Denoising in Representation Space
Xichen Pan, Aashu Singh, Satya Narayan Shukla, Xiangjun Fan, Shlok Kumar Mishra, Saining Xie
NeurIPS 2026

We repurpose a pretrained multimodal LLM as a noisy representation encoder, showing that MLLM priors transfer from understanding to denoising in representation space for text-to-image generation.

arXiv
Think Then Embed: Generative Context Improves Multimodal Embedding
Xuanming Cui, Jianpeng Cheng, Hong-you Chen, Satya Narayan Shukla, Abhijeet Awasthi, Xichen Pan, Chaitanya Ahuja, Shlok Kumar Mishra, Yonghuan Yang, Jun Xiao, Qi Guo, Ser-Nam Lim, Aashu Singh, Xiangjun Fan
ICLR 2026

A general framework for universal multimodal embedding: an MLLM reasoner first "thinks" through complex queries, then an embedder produces representations conditioned on the reasoning trace, reaching state-of-the-art on MMEB-V2.
