Shlok Mishra

I'm a Senior AI Research Scientist at Meta AI in Menlo Park, where I lead research on large-scale multimodal AI, spanning vision encoders, multimodal LLMs, image and video generation, and world models. My work focuses on the pre-training and post-training of large-scale vision and multimodal models, including vision encoders for generative multimodal systems.

Previously, I completed four research internships across Google DeepMind, Google Research, and Google Augmented Reality, working on self-supervised learning, generative modeling, representation learning, and controllable image generation. I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Maryland, College Park in 2023, where I was advised by Prof. David Jacobs and worked on self-supervised visual representation learning.

Research

I am interested in computer vision and machine learning. My current research focuses on multimodal foundation models: large-scale vision encoders, multimodal LLMs, and unified models for visual understanding and generation.

Unifying Contrastive and Generative Objectives for Visual Understanding and Text-to-Image Generation

NeurIPS 2026

We introduce DREAM, a unified framework that reconciles text-image contrastive and generative objectives via masking warmup, so a single encoder excels at both understanding and generation.
